NOCTIS is a small timber bedside companion with a 4-inch square touch screen. It replaces your phone at the bedside so the phone can stay in another room.
It wakes you gradually with screen light, rising sound and a quiet haptic pulse, then gives a short spoken briefing: the weather, your first calendar event and how long until it, and an estimate of your sleep and snoring from room sensing. It is not a medical device and does not track sleep stages.
The alarm is stored on the device, so it rings even if your Wi-Fi is down. A physical button stops or snoozes it without looking, and a physical switch turns the microphone off. Microphone audio is processed on the device and conversations are not stored.
No subscription, no ads, no feed. Setup is once, in a companion phone app, and your phone's own favourites setting can still let urgent calls from family ring through to the phone in the other room.
Try it at home for 60 days. If it does not suit you, send it back for a full refund.
