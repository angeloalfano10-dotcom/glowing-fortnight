## What would flip a no

- Proof that real people actually leave the phone in another room with it, plus a reliable way to still get emergency calls through to the bedside.
- A price near EUR 60 to 70, or proof that it works with my existing smart-home setup instead of sitting beside it.
- If it could reliably let urgent calls from the kids or family through without the phone being in the room, and cost around 70 euros.
- If it could reliably let a call from family or the kids' school ring through while the phone sits in another room, and I could see it working for a few weeks first, I'd reconsider.
- A reviewer or friend I trust showing it really got them to leave their phone outside the bedroom, or a lower price with a real return window.
- A price around EUR 70 to 80, or a trial period with free returns so I could find out whether I really leave the phone in another room.
- Seeing it work for a few weeks in a friend's home, with independent reviews showing the sleep and snoring estimate is useful, and a price closer to 80 euros.
- A price around 60 euros or a proper launch discount, plus some independent reviews showing it actually gets me out of bed.
- A price around EUR 70 to 80, or a trial period with free returns so I could see whether it actually keeps me off my phone at night.
- A price around 50 to 60 euros, plus a clear statement that the alarm works with no Wi-Fi.
- If it let urgent calls from a short list of people (family, school) come through, so I could really leave the phone in another room, and it was around EUR 80.
- A clear promise that the alarm works fully offline, plus a price closer to 60 euros, or a trial period with a real money-back return.
