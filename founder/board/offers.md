# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- Real, widely felt pain: people fall asleep scrolling and wake to notifications. The buyer is clearly named (design-aware adults with disposable income).
- The dream outcome is easy to say: less phone at night, a calmer morning. It is demoable, and a working UI prototype exists.
- Buyers with money and a taste for objects are reachable online. A waitlist is live (size unknown).
- Effort for the buyer is low in concept: set an alarm, put the phone elsewhere.
- Tight scope. No feeds, no medical claims. That keeps promises believable.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is unproven.** Nothing shows that people will actually leave the phone in another room or that this replaces it. Hardware arrives 2 Nov 2026, with no real-world proof yet. Commodity alarm clocks and smart displays already do alarm plus weather at lower prices.
2. **Price is not justified by the stack.** EUR 120 for an alarm and a briefing. The offer so far is features, not a stacked answer to the buyer's problems. The jump from EUR 89 to EUR 120 has no evidence behind it. Margin rests on an unverified under-EUR-40 cost, before shipping, returns, cloud TTS, payment fees and support.
3. **Effort and trust friction.** Setup needs a phone app, calendar OAuth and Wi-Fi, none finished. The phone-in-another-room sacrifice is large. No guarantee, no certification (CE/RED), no manufacturer, no funding.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many people are on the waitlist, and how many would pay EUR 120 today, with a deposit?
- What do you know that makes a buyer believe the phone really stays in the other room?
- Why not a EUR 20 alarm clock plus a phone in the hall?
- What is the guarantee, and can you afford returns at your real unit cost?
- What is the confirmed bill of materials, landed cost and ongoing per-user cloud cost?
- Could you double the price and still be the obvious choice? What justifies that?
- Who handles setup failures when calendar sync breaks at 6am?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Pre-orders or refundable deposits at EUR 120 from a meaningful number of strangers (threshold is yours to set, not assumed here).
- A costed BOM and landed-cost quote supporting the margin, including cloud TTS per user.
- A 30-day trial or money-back guarantee the numbers can absorb.
- Evidence from 10 or more real users that the phone stays out of the bedroom.
- A working end-to-end setup flow, and a named path to CE/RED compliance.
