# NOCTIS by offhours: one page

**Problem.** People fall asleep scrolling and wake to a screen of notifications because the phone is their alarm.
**Customer.** Design-aware adults, 25-45, Europe, who care about sleep and how their home looks.
**Offer and price.** A timber bedside companion that wakes you gently and gives a short morning briefing. EUR 120 incl. VAT. Alarm works offline, physical button and mic switch, no subscription, no ads, 60-day home trial, numbered founding batch of 300.

| Number | Value |
|---|---|
| Contribution per device | EUR 36.67 (37%) if the EUR 40 unit cost holds |
| Margin at plan | 7% |
| Break-even | 37 devices a month |
| Year 1 operating profit | EUR -2,559 (372 devices, assumption) |
| Startup spend | EUR 18,000 plus about EUR 12,000 working capital |
| Real 5-unit prototype cost | EUR 63.96 a unit (target EUR 40); contribution EUR 12.71 at that cost |
| Verdict | **Not yet** |

**Evidence.** Competitors (Loftie, Hatch, Echo, Nest) show demand for the category and a gap: no ads, no subscription, a considered object. Board: 2 fund-if, 1 pass (average 4.3 of 10). Simulated panel: 0 of 20 buy at EUR 120 before and after the improved offer; the acceptable range rose to about EUR 40-120. No real customers have been tested yet.
**What the money is for.** Not yet requested. First spend is a refundable-deposit test (16 Nov to 31 Dec 2026): success line 150 stranger deposits, then quotes, certification and a first batch.
**The ask.** Approval to run the deposit test and obtain three manufacturing quotes before any tooling money moves.
Simulated buyers are not customers; all costs are estimates. Not financial, legal or tax advice.
